bitdispatcher is installed

In Applications › bitdispatcher:

  • bitdispatcher opens the console in your browser.
  • Reset admin password sets a new password for admin.
  • Uninstall bitdispatcher removes it; the records are kept unless you choose otherwise twice.
  • bitdispatcher manual (PDF).

The browser warns about the certificate the first time: bitdispatcher makes its own until you give it one your network trusts (-tls-cert and -tls-key in the settings file).

Consoles on other computers open https://<this Mac>:8443/. If macOS's firewall is on, bitdispatcher was allowed through it.

Settings: /usr/local/etc/bitdispatcher.conf
Log: /Library/Logs/bitdispatcher/bitdispatcher.log
